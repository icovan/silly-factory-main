# Silly Factory

> Turn your nonsense into video. Drop a topic, let the machine surprise you.

---
## Let's be clear: this is not AI video generation

Them: image gen, video gen, 4K, photorealistic, cinematic lighting, cyberpunk, GPU on fire, gacha pulls.

Us: solid background. Big text. Typewriter. Robotic voice. 15 seconds. Done.

Visuals are rendered deterministically by code. No gacha, no GPU bills, no mysticism. The LLM does exactly one thing — it structures your topic into a script. The rest is code.

The style is locked. No font changes, no color changes, no pacing changes. **That's not a limitation, that's a feature.** You drop a topic, the factory decides the rest.

1. Open [https://203588.xyz](https://203588.xyz)
